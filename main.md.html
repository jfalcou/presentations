--- # Cayley-Dickson algebras --- ## Cayley-Dickson doubling ### Pairs of the previous algebra * Hamilton, 1837: $\mathbb{C}$ as pairs of reals. * Dickson, 1919: pairs over any algebra with a conjugation†. $$ (a,b)(c,d) = \left(ac - \overline{d}b,\, da + b\overline{c}\right), \qquad \overline{(a,b)} = \left(\overline{a}, -b\right) $$
| Algebra | Dimension | Element | Pairs of | |:------------:|:---------:|-----------------------------------------|:---------------:| | $\mathbb{R}$ | 1 | $a$ | | | $\mathbb{C}$ | 2 | $a + bi$ | $\mathbb{R}$ | | $\mathbb{H}$ | 4 | $a + bi + cj + dk$ | $\mathbb{C}$ | | $\mathbb{O}$ | 8 | $x_0 + x_1e_1 + \cdots + x_7e_7$ | $\mathbb{H}$ | | $\mathbb{S}$ | 16 | $x_0 + x_1e_1 + \cdots + x_{15}e_{15}$ | $\mathbb{O}$ |
† Sign conventions vary across the literature.

--- # Introducing kyosu --- ## kyosu
### Purpose * One type for every Cayley-Dickson algebra. * Complex numbers, quaternions, octonions. * Any dimension $2^n$. * Over `float`, `double`, or an `eve::wide`. * Exhaustive function set. * Named after their real counterparts. * With semantic options _à la_ EVE. * Covering all of libm, and more.
### Availability * Header-only C++20, BSL-1.0. * g++ 13 or clang++ 16, or above. * Dependency: [EVE](https://github.com/jfalcou/eve). * Sources: [github.com/jfalcou/kyosu](https://github.com/jfalcou/kyosu). * Documentation: [jfalcou.github.io/kyosu](https://jfalcou.github.io/kyosu).

--- ## Extension of real analytic functions
### Motivation * One complex implementation per function. * No specific implementation for quaternions and above. ### Real and pure parts 1. $z = z_0 + \underline{z}$, $\underline{z}$ the pure part. 2. $I_z = \underline{z} / |\underline{z}|$ squares to $-1$, as $i$. 3. $z = z_0 + |\underline{z}|\,I_z$: $z_0 + i\,|\underline{z}|$, $I_z$ for $i$. 4. So $z = \rho\,e^{\theta I_z}$, as in $\mathbb{C}$. ### Hypotheses * $f$ holomorphic, $f(\overline{w}) = \overline{f(w)}$. * Slice functions: Ghiloni and Perotti, 2011.

--- # SIMD support --- ## Registers of complex numbers
### EVE * C++20 SIMD types, functions, algorithms. * See: [github.com/jfalcou/eve](https://github.com/jfalcou/eve). * Instruction sets: * x86: SSE2 to AVX-512. * ARM: NEON, ASIMD, SVE of fixed size. * PowerPC: VMX, VSX. * RISC-V: RVV, in progress. * `eve::wide<T>`: a SIMD register of `T` values. * The operators of `T`, and masks for branches. * The register width of the target.
### eve::wide of kyosu::complex_t * Every kyosu function applies to each lane. * The source differs from the scalar one by its types only.
--- ## Register of a tuple ### Layout of eve::wide * For a tuple-like `T`: one register per element of `T`. * `std::complex<float>[4]` interleaves the parts. * `eve::wide<kyosu::complex_t<float>>`. * One register of real parts. * One register of imaginary parts. --- ## kyosu and std::simd
### std::simd ([P2663R7](https://wg21.link/p2663r7)) * `std::simd::vec<std::complex<float>>`. * Parts interleaved in adjacent lanes. * Loads `std::complex` arrays as they are. * The functions of `<complex>`. * Complex instructions where they exist. * AVX512-FP16, Neon v8.3.
### kyosu (C++20) * `eve::wide<kyosu::complex_t<float>>`. * One register per part. * No permute, a deinterleave on load. * Every dimension, two hundred functions. * Every target of EVE. * x86, ARM, PowerPC.
### Status of std::simd, November 2026 * P2663R7: in the C++26 draft since the Hagenberg meeting, February 2025. * libstdc++ 16: `std::simd`, experimental, without `std::complex` lanes. * libc++: no `std::simd` yet ([#118384](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/issues/118384), [#127882](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/issues/127882)). --- ## One source, scalar or register ### Generic code

--- # Conclusion --- ## Conclusion
### std::complex * Interleaved parts, one value at a time. * caxpy: the Annex G call keeps the loop scalar. * `-fcx-limited-range`: * caxpy ×4.3 to ×6.2, Mandelbrot unchanged.
### kyosu * One type, one product, every dimension. * Exhaustive function set, scalar or register. * Reference kernels: ×1.9 to ×10.8. * Mandelbrot set: ×3.5 to ×13.2.
